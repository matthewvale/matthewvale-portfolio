Out of Mind

The world only exists while you're looking at it.

  • Your light builds the map as you walk. Anything you stop seeing forgets itself and regrows differently.
  • Ghosts only move while unseen. Pick up snowflakes, then click or tap a ghost in your light to freeze it. Walk into a frozen ghost to shatter it for gold.
  • Stand still for five seconds and your light blinks, giving every ghost a free step.
  • Find the green door. Spend gold at the shrine between levels.
  • Keep an eye out for a pumpkin.
